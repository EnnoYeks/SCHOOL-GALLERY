Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload content?

You can upload photos and videos from your profile. Navigate to the gallery and click on the upload button. Make sure your file is under 100MB.

Is my privacy protected?

Yes! We take privacy seriously. You can control who sees your content through privacy settings. Student data is never shared with third parties.

Can I delete my posts?

Absolutely! You can delete any post you've created anytime. Just navigate to your post and click the delete option.

What file formats are supported?

We support PNG, JPG, GIF for images and MP4, WebM for videos. Maximum file size is 100MB per upload.
